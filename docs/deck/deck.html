Foreman · 04 · How it works

Six steps.
Two are neural.

01
Detect
PatchCore, trained on good parts only, scores each part and draws the heatmap.
02
Fuse
Press sensors against the machine's baseline. Statistics, not an LLM.
03
Retrieve
Manual sections that match the signature, plus the repairs they point to.
04
Reason
Gemma 4 vision reads photo, heatmap, sensors and manual. Structured diagnosis.
05
Verify
Every manual citation is checked against what the model was shown.
06
Approve
A supervisor approves or rejects. The repair runs, the sensors recover.
